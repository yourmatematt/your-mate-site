# Your Mate Agency - SEO Audit & Improvement Plan

**Audit Date:** January 2026
**Site:** yourmateagency.com.au
**Overall Score:** 7.5/10

---

## Executive Summary

Your Mate Agency has a solid SEO foundation with excellent schema markup, authentic keyword usage, and strong local targeting. However, there are significant opportunities to improve visibility through structure fixes, content expansion, and featured snippet optimization.

**Key Strengths:**
- Comprehensive schema markup (LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Service, Article)
- Natural keyword integration without stuffing
- Strong local geographic targeting (Gippsland, regional Victoria)
- Authentic, conversational content that builds trust
- Clean, SEO-friendly URL structure

**Critical Gaps:**
- Blog post header hierarchy issues
- Missing BreadcrumbList schema
- Under-optimized About page
- Limited internal linking between content
- Missing location-specific landing pages
- Content not formatted for featured snippets
