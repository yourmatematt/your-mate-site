EASTER IN MALLACOOTA 2026

24 March 2026

By Matt Rowlands, Your Mate

THE GUIDE

Heading to Mallacoota for the Easter long weekend? Good call. This little coastal town in East Gippsland is one of Victoria's best-kept secrets — tucked between the inlet, the ocean, and the national park. Whether you're coming for a few days or the whole week, here's everything that's happening and everything you need to know.

WHAT'S ON

Mallacoota Easter Market
Saturday 4th April | Lions Park | 9am – 1pm

The Easter market is on at Lions Park with local stalls, food, and plenty to browse. If you've been to a Mallacoota market before, you know the vibe — laid back, local, and worth rocking up early for.

Nanny Rae Rae will be at the market with her handmade pieces — if you want a preview of what she'll have on the day, check out her collection online.

Face Painting & Easter Photo Booth
Saturday 4th April | The Mudbrick | 9am – 1pm

One for the kids. Face painting and an Easter photo booth set up out the front of the Mudbrick. Runs alongside the market so you can do both in one lap.

The Chris Cross Clothing Sale
Easter Saturday & Sunday, 4th & 5th April | The Mudbrick | 9am – 4pm

A Mallacoota institution for 11 years. Thousands of preloved quality items — dresses, jackets, skirts, scarves, shoes, jewellery, handbags, sizes 8 to 20+. If you love a good rummage, this is your weekend. Contact Christine on 0417 321 388 for details or if you want to sell your own clothes.

Scallywags Steak Night
Thursday 2nd April

Kick off the long weekend early with Steak Night at Scallywags. Then come back Friday 3rd April for meat tray raffles and happy hour — $5 beer and wine from 5pm to 6pm (Friday Only).

WHAT TO DO

Get Out on the Water

The inlet is what makes Mallacoota special. Grab a boat and explore at your own pace — fish, swim, find a quiet beach, or just drift around with a coldie. No licence needed for either option.

Mallacoota Barbie Boats — electric barbecue boats that fit the whole family. Float around the inlet, cook lunch on board, and find your own little beach to pull up on.

Hire a boat →

Walk the Tracks

Some of the best coastal walks in Victoria are right here:

  • Captain Stevenson's Point — easy loop with massive views over the inlet and ocean
  • Betka Beach — a short drive from town, great surf beach and rock pools for the kids
  • Gabo Island — if you're up for an adventure, there are boat trips out to the lighthouse. Penguins, seals, and one of the most remote lighthouses in Australia

ESSENTIAL INFO

Getting There

From Melbourne: 6 to 7 hours via Princes Highway through Lakes Entrance and Cann River, depending on stops with the little ones. The last stretch through the national park is beautiful but windy — take it easy.
From Sydney: About 6.5 hours down through Eden and the Sapphire Coast.
From Canberra: About 5 hours via Cooma and Bombala.
