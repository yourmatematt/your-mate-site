SEO & Google

Do I need SEO for my local business?

If you want people to find you when they search for what you do in your area, then yes. When someone searches "electrician Bairnsdale" or "cafe Lakes Entrance", you want to show up. Local SEO makes that happen. It's not magic — it's just making sure Google knows what you do and where you do it.

What's Google Business Profile and why does it matter?

It's the box that shows up on the right side of Google when people search for your business, and how you appear on Google Maps. For local businesses, it's often more important than your actual website. It shows your hours, photos, reviews, and contact info right there in the search results. I can set it up properly and keep it optimised so you show up when it counts. Learn more in my guide to Google Maps for local businesses.

How do I get my business to show up on Google Maps?

You need a Google Business Profile set up and verified, then optimised with the right categories, photos, and information. Having consistent details (name, address, phone) across the web helps too. Good reviews don't hurt either. I can handle all of this for you — it's one of those things that's easy when you know how, frustrating when you don't.

AI & Automation

What is AI consultancy?

It's helping you figure out where AI can actually save you time or money in your business. Not the hype and buzzwords — practical things like chatbots that answer customer questions, systems that handle bookings, or tools that help with content. I cut through the noise and show you what's actually useful for your specific situation.

How can AI help my small business?

Depends on your business. Common wins: chatbots that capture leads and answer questions 24/7, automated booking systems that stop double-bookings, help writing content for your website or socials, or handling repetitive admin tasks. The key is finding what actually saves you time without overcomplicating things or costing a fortune.

Is AI going to replace my website?

No. AI changes how people find and interact with businesses, but you still need a home base online. Your website is where people go to learn about you, see your work, check your credentials, and get in touch. What AI does is make your website work harder — helping visitors find what they need and turning them into customers. They work together, not instead of each other.
