### Getting There

**From Melbourne:** About 3 to 3.5 hours to Bairnsdale via the M1 and Princes Highway. Easy drive — straight shot through Traralgon and Sale.

**From Lakes Entrance:** About 30 minutes west along the Princes Highway to Bairnsdale.

**From Sydney:** About 7 hours via the Princes Highway through Eden, or cut inland via Canberra.

**From Canberra:** About 4.5 hours via Cooma and the Monaro Highway.

### Weather

It's winter in Gippsland. Expect cold mornings (2-8 degrees), mild days (12-16 degrees), and a solid chance of rain. **Bring layers, a decent jacket, and shoes that can handle wet ground.** If you're coming from Melbourne, think a step colder and a step wetter.
