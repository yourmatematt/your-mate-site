If you've got your own tinny, the Lakes are perfect for a day's exploring. Head up to Metung or across to Raymond Island — the koalas are still there in winter and you'll practically have the place to yourself.

### Walk the Tracks

Some of the best walks in Gippsland are right here, and winter is the best time to do them — cooler temps, no crowds, and the bush looks incredible after rain.

- **The Entrance Walking Track** — follows the coastline from town to the actual entrance where the lakes meet the ocean. Easy enough for kids, and the views are worth it.
- **Colquhoun Forest** — if you want something a bit more rugged. Mountain bike trails and walking tracks through the bush.
- **Nyerimilang Heritage Park** — about 15 minutes from town. Heritage homestead, gardens, and views over the lakes. Great for a picnic even in winter.
- **Kalimna Jetty Walk** — easy family walk through the bush to a jetty on the lake. Quiet, flat, and good for little legs.

### Go Fishing

This is what Lakes Entrance is famous for. Winter is prime time for bream in the lakes, and the offshore season for tuna and kingfish can still be firing.

- **From the Footbridge** — the classic Lakes spot. Grab some bait from the tackle shop and drop a line. Kids love it.
- **North Arm** — flathead and bream. Accessible by boat or by walking track.
