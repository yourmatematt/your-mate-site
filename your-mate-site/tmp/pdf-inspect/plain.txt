The Google Business Profile
Checklist for Accommodation
Operators
25 things to fix on your Google listing so guests book direct — not through
Booking.com.
Booking.com and the other OTAs charge you 15–25% per booking. That's $25,000+ a
year for a small motel like the one we work with up the road in Mallacoota.
Your Google Business Profile is the single biggest free tool you have to win those
bookings back. Most operators have it set up wrong — or barely set up at all. This
checklist fixes that.
Tick each box as you go. It'll take you about an hour. Print it, scribble on it, do
whatever works.
1. Foundation
THE BASICS — GET THESE WRONG AND NOTHING ELSE MATTERS
Claim and verify your listing
If Google doesn't trust you own the business, you can't edit anything. Search your business
name on Google and look for "Own this business?"
Business name matches your real signage
No keyword stuffing. "Sunset Motel Best Beach Accommodation" gets you suspended.
Address is correct and consistent
It must match exactly what's on your website, your invoices, and any directory listings.
Phone number is your direct line
Not a forwarding number, not Booking.com's tracking line. Your number, that you answer.

Website link points to your own site
Not your Booking.com page. Your domain, your booking engine.
2. Categories & Attributes
TELL GOOGLE EXACTLY WHAT YOU ARE
Primary category set correctly
"Motel," "Hotel," "Holiday home rental agency," "Bed & Breakfast," "Cabin rental agency" —
pick the most specific one.
Add 2–4 secondary categories
Add things like "Tourist attraction," "Wedding venue," "Conference centre" if they apply. Don't
pad with stuff that isn't true.
Tick every relevant amenity
Wi-Fi, parking, pet-friendly, family rooms, pool, BBQ, kitchenette — guests filter by these.
Set accessibility attributes honestly
Step-free entrance, accessible parking, accessible bathroom. Travellers actively search for
these.
3. Hours & Contact
NO SURPRISES FOR GUESTS
Check-in and check-out hours set
If guests can call 24/7, set hours to "Open 24 hours." If not, list reception hours.
Public holidays and seasonal closures added
Use the "Special hours" feature for Easter, Christmas, off-season closures.
Description fills the full 750 characters
Plain English. Mention your town, what makes you different, and what guests get.
4. Photos
THIS IS WHERE MOST OPERATORS LOSE THE BOOKING

Logo uploaded (square, high-res)
Square format, minimum 720x720px.
Cover photo is your best exterior shot
Daylight, wide angle, no people walking through the frame.
At least 10 interior photos uploaded
Every room type, bathroom, kitchen if you have one. Beds made, blinds open, lights on.
Outdoor and surrounds covered
Pool, garden, beach access, view from the balcony — what people are actually buying.
Add a new photo at least monthly
Google rewards active listings. Even a sunset shot from the deck counts.
5. The Direct Booking Bit
WHERE THE COMMISSION SAVINGS ACTUALLY HAPPEN
Reservation link goes to your own booking engine
In the GBP dashboard, set "Reservations" to your direct booking URL — not Booking.com or
Airbnb.
Menu / services link added (if applicable)
For motels with restaurants or accommodation packages, list them under "Services."
Messaging turned on
Lets guests text you directly from search results. Big trust signal. Be ready to reply within a few
hours.
Why this matters
Google now lets travellers book direct from the search results. If your reservation link
points at your own site, you keep 100% of that booking. If it points at Booking.com,
you've just paid commission on a booking Google sent you for free.
6. Reviews
YOUR MOST POWERFUL CONVERSION TOOL

================================================================================
Reply to every review — good and bad
Within 48 hours. Personalised, by name. Never copy-paste the same reply.
Set up a review request system
Send guests your Google review link by SMS or email the morning of checkout. Aim for one
new review per week minimum.
Get your short Google review link
In your GBP dashboard, click "Ask for reviews" — copy that link, save it, use it everywhere.
7. Posts & Q&A
FREE REAL ESTATE MOST OPERATORS IGNORE
Post an update every 2–4 weeks
Local events, seasonal specials, new amenities, photos of the area. Counts as a fresh signal to
Google.
Pre-load the Q&A section
Add the questions guests actually ask — pet policy, parking, late check-in — and answer them
yourself. Saves you replying to the same enquiry 50 times.
