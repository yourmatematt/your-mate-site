About the course

What you'll get out of it

A practical, five-week course to help you establish a professional online presence — or fix the one you've got. No jargon, no theory — just the stuff that actually makes a difference to how customers find you and what they see when they do.

Facilitated by Matt Rowlands from Your Mate Agency — a Mallacoota-based web designer and digital strategist who works with small businesses across Gippsland and regional Victoria.

Completion bonus: Complete all 5 sessions and receive a free Digital Health Check — a personalised audit of your online presence with specific recommendations for your business.

The 5 sessions

What we'll cover

Session 1

Getting Found

Set up or fix your Google Business Profile and get found when people search for your business.

Session 2

What Good Looks Like

Get your business social media set up properly and see what good (and bad) websites actually look like.

Session 3

Working Smarter

Learn how to use AI to write posts, respond to reviews, and create content in minutes instead of hours.

Session 4

Your Online Toolkit

Figure out if you need a website, learn Canva, and make sure your online presence is consistent.

Session 5

Staying Visible

Understand what SEO actually means and build a weekly routine that takes 30 minutes.
